An ICU bedside monitor driven by my real GitHub activity. It behaves like a real heart: it rests while I'm idle, speeds up while I work, and recovers afterwards. Updates every ~5 minutes via GitHub Actions.

How to read my vitals
  • HR (BPM)

    Live effort. Rests at ~70. Every recent event (push, PR, review, issue) ramps HR up toward ~130 within minutes, and it recovers over ~35 minutes after I stop

  • SpO2 (%)

    Oxygen saturation. Healthy 97-99%, dips about 1% at full effort. Consistency (active days) nudges it slightly

  • NIBP (mmHg)

    Blood pressure. ~118/76 at rest. Systolic rises up to ~148 while working, diastolic barely moves

  • RESP (rpm)

    Breathing. 14 at rest, up to 24 while working. The breathing sway on the ECG follows it

  • HRV (RMSSD ms)

    Variability. 35-60 ms at rest depending on consistency, drops while working

  • NEWS2

    Overall early-warning score. The NHS NEWS2 banding applied to HR, SpO2, systolic BP and RR

Rhythms: normal sinus rhythm at rest, tachycardia while working hard, then back to normal. After 2+ days of no activity: bradycardia → irregular rhythm (PVCs) → asystole after 7 days with no activity.

Alarms: NEWS2 5-6 = urgent review, NEWS2 7+ = escalate. The bottom strip shows my last 30 days of contributions.
